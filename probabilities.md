# Transition Probabilities to Natural Language Mapping

## `pinf` - Susceptibility to first-contact persuasion
| Value | Level | Description |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| ≤ 0.05 | **Low** | You are highly resistant to new claims and rarely change your mind based on a single encounter. |
| 0.10 | **Moderate** | You are somewhat open to new information, but a single message is not enough to fully convince you. |
| ≥ 0.15 | **High** | You are quite impressionable and can be convinced by a compelling message on first contact. |

---

## `pmd` - Tendency to become sceptical on first contact
| Value | Level | Description |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| ≤ 0.05 | **Low** | You do not tend to develop scepticism readily - exposure to a claim does not typically make you dismissive of it. |
| ≥ 0.10 | **High** | You are quick to become sceptical: once you encounter a claim and resist it, you actively discount it going forward. |

> *No distinct moderate label; the two-value grid maps directly to low / high.*

---

## `pad` - Willingness to flip opinion under disagreement
| Value | Level | Description |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| ≤ 0.05 | **Low** | When you disagree with a message, you almost never change your position - you hold your ground firmly. |
| 0.10 | **Moderate** | Encountering disagreement occasionally causes you to reconsider and adjust your stance. |
| ≥ 0.15 | **High** | You are sensitive to opposing views: disagreement with a message can lead you to adopt the other side's position. |

---

## `popi` - Tendency to reinforce one's own opinion
| Value | Level | Description |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| ≤ 0.10 | **Low** | Agreement with a message does not particularly strengthen your existing beliefs. |
| 0.15 / 0.20 | **Moderate** | When you agree with a message or successfully resist a challenge, your convictions are moderately reinforced. |
| ≥ 0.25 | **High** | Confirmation of your views or resisting a contrary message significantly deepens your commitment to your current position. |

---

## `prd` - Active reading / message-processing rate
| Value | Level | Description |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| ≤ 0.10 | **Low** | You read and process incoming messages slowly, engaging with only a small fraction of what reaches you. |
| 0.20 / 0.30 | **Moderate** | You read messages at an average pace, engaging with a reasonable share of the information flow. |
| ≥ 0.40 | **High** | You are a highly active reader who processes a large proportion of incoming messages quickly. |
